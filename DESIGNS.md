# Website layout prototypes (Canva)

Ten earthy website layouts for the Science Day project: which mosquito repellent
(chemical vs natural) works best. Each is a 1920x1080 landscape (full-screen website
ratio) design with 3 scrolling screens: Hero, Methods, Results.
All numbers are **test data** until real data is added.

## Test data used

| Method | Type | Effective |
|--------|------|-----------|
| 1 Chemical spray (DEET) | Chemical | 92% |
| 2 Citronella candle | Natural | 58% |
| 3 Neem oil lotion | Natural | 74% |
| 4 Lemon eucalyptus oil | Natural | 81% |
| 5 Mosquito coil | Chemical | 66% |

Chemicals tested: DEET, Picaridin, Permethrin. Image boxes are placeholders.
